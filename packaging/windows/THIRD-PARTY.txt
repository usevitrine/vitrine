Vitrine for Windows comes with these programs, unchanged, in the tools folder. Each is free software under the
GNU Lesser General Public License (LGPL); their licenses are in this folder. You may replace them with your own
builds (point ffmpeg, ffprobe and vipsthumbnail in config.toml at them).

FFmpeg 8.1.3 (tools\ffmpeg) - https://ffmpeg.org
  The LGPL build by BtbN: https://github.com/BtbN/FFmpeg-Builds (release autobuild-2026-09-30-13-08,
  ffmpeg-n8.1.3-9-g29e619e767-win64-lgpl-shared-8.1). Its source code and the build scripts are at those addresses:
  https://github.com/FFmpeg/FFmpeg/tree/n8.1.3 and https://github.com/BtbN/FFmpeg-Builds
  License: ffmpeg-LICENSE.txt

libvips 8.18.7 (tools\vips) - https://www.libvips.org
  The official "web" build: https://github.com/libvips/build-win64-mxe/releases/tag/v8.18.7
  (vips-dev-x64-web-8.18.7-static). Source code: https://github.com/libvips/libvips/tree/v8.18.7 and the build
  scripts at https://github.com/libvips/build-win64-mxe; the libraries it includes and their versions are listed in
  libvips-versions.json.
